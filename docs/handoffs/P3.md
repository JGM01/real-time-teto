# Handoff: English CVVC Speech Pipeline (P3 Next)

Status: P1, P2, and P3 are complete. P4 is next.

Current speech contracts, qualification commands, source pins, and the explicit
OpenUtau coverage repairs are documented in `docs/speech.md`. The sections below
retain the P3 input requirements. The implementation uses the direct Rust GRU.
The pinned checkpoint has 256 hidden values and 768 gate values.

## Fixed decisions

- Bank: `TETO-English-150401.zip`.
- Voicebank: Kasane Teto English CVVC, user-supplied, personal prototype.
- Code license: MIT OR Apache-2.0.
- Language: English only. No Japanese, Katakana, mora, or Japanese front end.
- Prosody: rule-based first. FS2 is optional later. It is not part of P3.
- Delivery window: configurable, default one; two-clause window is implemented
  and unit-tested. Audio gap measurement is later.
- Bank files are not committed. The archive is at
  `voicebanks/TETO-English-150401.zip` and is ignored by git.

Bank facts:

- Official URL:
  https://kasaneteto.jp/assets/download/utau/TETO-English-150401.zip
- SHA-256:
  `addb3ab9dbe3dce7cb40fe6ba0c93ab5814d02acf5b091d0a762de370b813d6e`
- 558 WAV files, mono, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM.
- 2,681 OTO alias rows.
- 1,032 seconds of audio.
- Aliases are X-SAMPA/CVVC. They include `@` and `{`. Do not assume ARPAbet
  aliases.
- The bank has a Teto-specific missing `V` sample. OpenUtau uses `V=@`.

The bank is not open source. Its own terms apply. Do not add the archive,
WAVs, FRQ files, derived packs, or golden Teto audio to the repository.

Other phase details:

- **P4:** implement `RuleBasedProsody` and `PsolaRenderer`. Select OTO units
  from the real bank, preserve consonant transients, render voiced regions with
  pitch marks, use 44.1 kHz native rate, and cache 32 or 64 MiB of units.
  Produce offline PCM and run golden, property and listening gates. Do not
  train a model.
- **P5:** add the `rtrb` PCM boundary, a CPAL output probe, and an Apple
  `AVAudioPlayerNode` sink using `.dataPlayedBack`. Keep 5,760-frame capacity
  and a 1,920-frame start threshold as initial values; test 256- and 512-frame
  callbacks. Acknowledge a whole clause only after device completion. Add the
  epoch, cancellation and route fence tests.
- **P6:** the two-clause mailbox window is already implemented behind
  `DeliveryWindow`. Add a nonblocking client dispatcher so a pull can wait
  while playback receipts flow. Measure the device inter-clause gap with one
  and two clauses. Keep the default at one until the measurement passes.
- **P7:** qualify the real LLM-to-speech path on the desktop and the iPhone 15
  Pro Max. Measure first audible sample, starvation, complete audio latency,
  memory, thermal state, offline behavior and the bank terms. Do not reuse
  text-only latency as audio evidence.
- **P8:** consider FS2 or another neural prosody model only if rule-based
  quality fails the listening gate. There is no compatible pretrained adaptor.
  Training, export, rights and latency are separate gates.

## Research carried forward

The full research memo was removed. These are the facts that still affect the
plan.

### Bank and language

- The chosen bank is Teto English CVVC, not the Japanese bank.
- The bank is X-SAMPA/CVVC, not ARPAbet.
- The bank is 44.1 kHz mono 16-bit, 558 WAVs, 2,681 OTO rows, 1,032 seconds.
- The bank has a Teto-specific missing `V` sample; OpenUtau maps `V=@`.
- English only. No Japanese phones, mora, Katakana or Japanese front end.
- The bank is singing material. Speech naturalness is unproven.
- The bank terms are separate from the code license. Do not redistribute the
  archive or derived audio. Terms:
  https://kasaneteto.jp/guidelines/voice.html

### Phonemization

- Use the direct CMU dictionary data. Its license permits commercial use with
  attribution. Do not use the GPL Python `cmudict` wrapper.
- CMUdict supplies stress, not syllable boundaries.
- g2p_en is Apache-2.0 and uses NumPy inference. There is no official ONNX
  export.
- Export parity must preserve GRU gate order, reset, EOS and greedy ties.
- Try `tract-onnx 0.21.19` CPU first. `ort 2.0.0-rc.13` is the alternative;
  rc.15 requires Rust 1.92.
- Do not ship eSpeak, Praat, KAKASI or the GPL Piper successor.

### CVVC plan

- OpenUtau `EnXSampaPhonemizer` and `SyllableBasedPhonemizer` are the reference.
  They are MIT.
- Alias patterns are contextual: `- cv`, `- ccv`, `v c`, `v v`, `c c`,
  `v c t`, `_cv`, `c cv`, with space and no-space variants.
- The planner needs syllables and word consonant context.
- Missing aliases must fail with `MissingUnit`; never drop a phone.

### Audio and receipts

- One synthesis worker owns text, prosody and rendering. One PCM ring buffer
  feeds the device. Do not add a queue per text stage.
- The current one-clause delivery window is the default. The two-clause window
  is an experiment; do not acknowledge queued or partially played audio.
- Acknowledge each whole clause only after device playback completion.
- CPAL playback timestamps are predictions. Use Apple
  `AVAudioPlayerNode` `.dataPlayedBack` for production receipts.
- The audio callback performs no allocation, locks, I/O, logging, model work
  or unbounded work.

### Limits

- Text-only evidence: desktop first-clause p95 450 ms; phone p95 816 ms.
- These are not audio latencies. First audible sample, starvation and complete
  audio remain pending.
- First-audio work must be measured on the real Teto path. Synthetic audio
  cannot qualify it.

## P2 input surface

`teto-speech` is English-only. The default build has no ONNX, audio, WAV, or

Existing modules:

Existing boundary:

```text
SpeechInput
  -> PhonemeSequence { phones, words, syllables, breaks, frontend_revision }
  -> UnitPlan { units, aliases, timing, prosody }
```

`PhonemeSequence::validate` already checks bounds and spans. `SpeechInput`
checks empty text, control characters and 240-scalar input.

The P2 input contained these `todo!()` seams:

P3 implements the first six. It leaves prosody, PSOLA and device output at
their seams. P3 stops at a valid `UnitPlan`.

## P3 scope

### 1. Bank import and alias inventory

- Read the official archive or an extracted directory.
- Parse `oto.ini`. Handle encoding, duplicates, signed cutoff and path
  traversal.
- Read WAV headers. Validate 44.1 kHz, mono, 16-bit.
- Preserve each alias, source file, offset, preutterance, overlap, cutoff,
  optional consonant velocity and pitch group.
- Compute a revision hash over the imported manifest.
- Build an `AliasInventoryId` over the alias set.
- Report aliases that do not map to English phones.
- Do not decode all PCM at import time. Record source ranges and metadata.
- Add an import report with counts, missing aliases and duplicate aliases.

The bank may use multiple pitch groups. Preserve them. Do not flatten pitch
groups silently.

### 2. CMUdict and OOV phonemization

- Use the direct CMU dictionary data, not the GPL Python `cmudict` package.
- Pin the source revision and hash. The researched revision is
  `74790861f652b15e4ac49015a90074ad62a27690`.
- Parse pronunciations, stress, alternate pronunciations and comments.
- Support overrides and a documented duplicate policy.
- Preserve multiple pronunciations. Mark ambiguity instead of hiding it.
- Keep text byte spans and phone spans.
- Do not run number or abbreviation expansion again. The LLM already
  normalized the text.
- The g2p_en OOV path:
  - The inspected implementation is NumPy, not ONNX.
  - Build a NumPy oracle first.
  - Export encoder plus one decoder step, or implement the bounded GRU
    directly in Rust.
  - Use ONNX opset 13 first.
  - Try `tract-onnx 0.21.19` CPU first.
  - Keep `ort 2.0.0-rc.13` as the alternative. Do not use rc.15: it requires
    Rust 1.92.
  - Set `MAX_WORD_ASCII = 64` and `MAX_G2P_STEPS = 20`.
  - Reject a missing EOS with `DecoderLimit`.
  - Compare exact greedy token sequences with the NumPy oracle.
- Do not use eSpeak in the shipped path. It is GPLv3.

Sources:

- CMUdict: https://github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict
- g2p_en: https://github.com/Kyubyong/g2p
- OpenUtau ARPAbet G2P:
  https://github.com/openutau/OpenUtau/tree/master/OpenUtau.Core/G2p

### 3. Syllable segmentation

- CMUdict supplies stress, not syllable boundaries.
- Implement a deterministic English syllabifier over the phone sequence.
- Fill `Syllable` spans: onset, nucleus, coda, stress.
- Fill `LexicalWord.syllables` and `PhonemeSequence.syllables`.
- Validate every span with `PhonemeSequence::validate`.
- Add properties for monophthongs, diphthongs, clusters, `ng`, `th`, `sh`,
  `ch`, `jh`, `zh`, `r`, `l`, `w`, `y`, final consonants and apostrophes.
- Mark uncertain syllables in diagnostics. Do not silently drop a phone.

### 4. ARPAbet to X-SAMPA

Implement the mapping in `TetoXsampaMap`. Start from the OpenUtau mapping:

```text
aa=A  ae={  ah=V  ao=O  aw=aU  ax=@  ay=aI
b=b   ch=tS d=d   dh=D  dx=4  eh=E  el=@l
em=@m en=@n eng=@N er=3 ey=eI f=f  g=g
hh=h  ih=I  iy=i jh=dZ k=k   l=l  m=m
n=n   ng=N  ow=oU oy=OI p=p   q=・ r=r
s=s   sh=S  t=t   th=T  uh=U  uw=u v=v
w=w   y=j   z=z   zh=Z
```

Teto exception: map `V` to `@` when the bank lacks `V`. Keep the exception
bank-specific and tested.

The mapping is a bank boundary. Do not store X-SAMPA in the lexical phone
types. `PhonemeSequence` stays ARPAbet.

### 5. CVVC alias planner

Port the behavior of OpenUtau:

- `EnXSampaPhonemizer.cs`
  https://github.com/openutau/OpenUtau/blob/master/OpenUtau.Plugin.Builtin/EnXSampaPhonemizer.cs
- `SyllableBasedPhonemizer.cs`
  https://github.com/openutau/OpenUtau/blob/master/OpenUtau.Plugin.Builtin/SyllableBasedPhonemizer.cs

The port is MIT. Keep attribution in `THIRD_PARTY.md` or the file header.

Required behavior:

- Starting vowel: `- v`, fallback `v`.
- Starting CV: `- cv`, `c v`, `cv`.
- Starting cluster: `- ccv`, `_cv`, `c v`, `cv`, and consonant fallbacks.
- VV: `v v`, `vv`, diphthong split, glide fallbacks.
- VCV: `v cv`, `v c v`, `v ccv`, `c v`.
- VC endings: `v c`, `v c t`, `v c-`, `c-`.
- CC endings and clusters: `c c`, `c c t`, `v c c`, `v c c t`.
- Space and no-space aliases.
- Affricate and diphthong splitting when the combined alias is missing.
- Teto `V=@`.
- Velar nasal fallback, dark L and other bank-specific rules only when the
  bank lacks the preferred alias.
- Deterministic selection. If several aliases match, use a documented order.
- Never silently drop a phone. Return `MissingUnit` when no alias exists.
- Record substitutions in the unit plan or a diagnostic.

The planner must use the OTO manifest and pitch group. Do not assume every
alias exists. The bank is a finite recording set.

### 6. Diagnostics

Add bounded diagnostics for:

- OOV count and word byte spans.
- Ambiguous pronunciations.
- Missing aliases.
- Alias substitutions.
- Pitch fallback.
- Syllable uncertainty.

Do not log clause text by default.

## Tests and gates

P3 is done when these pass:

1. `cargo test -p teto-speech`
2. `cargo test -p teto-speech --features llm-adapter`
3. `cargo test -p teto-llm-client`
4. `make check`
5. OTO import on the real bank, run locally and not committed.
6. Alias coverage report on at least 1,000 assistant-like clauses, names and
   technical terms. Every phone in every selected word must map or return a
   typed error.
7. OpenUtau parity on a fixed 50-clause set: same alias sequence for every
   syllable, or a documented allowed substitution.
8. CMU/G2P tests:
   - exact NumPy parity for the OOV model;
   - held-out name/technical set;
   - heteronym diagnostics;
   - stress preservation;
   - no silent truncation.
9. Syllable property tests:
   - every phone belongs to exactly one syllable;
   - onset, nucleus and coda are within the syllable;
   - no empty nucleus for a vowel;
   - spans survive `PhonemeSequence::validate`.
10. Fuzz the OTO parser and the alias planner with malformed aliases, encoding,
    duplicate rows, huge offsets and reversed spans. No panic, no path
    traversal, no unbounded allocation.
11. Default `cargo tree -p teto-speech` has no ONNX, audio or voicebank
    dependency.
12. The bank archive and extracted audio are absent from git.

## Suggested implementation order

1. OTO/WAV import and manifest hashing. This makes the real alias inventory
   visible.
2. Alias coverage report. This tells us which mapping rules the bank needs.
3. X-SAMPA map and Teto `V=@`.
4. CMUdict parser and lexicon.
5. Syllabifier.
6. g2p_en oracle and OOV backend.
7. CVVC planner.
8. OpenUtau parity and listening smoke.

Do not write the CVVC planner before inspecting the real alias inventory. The
bank's aliases decide the rule set.

## Risks

- The OTO alias inventory is the source of truth. Inspect it first.
- The Teto bank may have multiple pitches and missing aliases.
- The OpenUtau phonemizer is large and case-heavy. Port behavior and test
  against it; do not rewrite it from intuition.
- CMUdict does not resolve heteronyms by itself.
- g2p_en has no official ONNX model.
- The bank is singing material. Speech naturalness is still unproven.
- The bank license is separate from the code license.

## Definition of done

A `PhonemeSequence` from English text and a `UnitPlan` from the real Teto
English bank, with deterministic alias selection, typed failures for missing
units, OTO provenance, tests and coverage reports. No audio is required for P3.
